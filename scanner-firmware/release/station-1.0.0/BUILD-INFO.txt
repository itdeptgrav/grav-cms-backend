and records, or simply rebuild from the sketch and upload that binary. Either
way the version typed into the form must be exactly 1.0.0.

What it is: a hand-scanner device paired (by QR) to the Ironing or Trimming
dashboard as its remote input. See FIRMWARE-README.md.
